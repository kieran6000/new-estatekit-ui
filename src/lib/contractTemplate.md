# EstateKit Service Agreement

### At a glance

This page sums up the agreement in plain English. If it ever differs from the terms that follow, the terms win.

- **What we do:** run your Facebook and Instagram ads, build your landing page and qualification form, send every qualified lead to your WhatsApp, and give you a dashboard, weekly updates and a weekly check-in form.
- **What you do:** call your leads fast, follow up properly, mark each lead's outcome on the dashboard, keep your ad spend running and fill in your weekly check-in form.
- **Your areas:** while this agreement runs, we don't run seller ads for any other agent in your target suburbs.
- **What it costs:** our management fee, plus ad spend you pay to Meta. We never take a cut of your commission.
- **Our guarantee:** at least 60 qualified seller leads in your first 90 days, or we keep managing your ads for free until you get them.
- **Privacy:** we keep your business information confidential, only use the access you give us to run your campaign, and remove it when we're done.
- **Your data:** you own your leads, your Facebook page, your Instagram and your ad account.

### What we do

For your target suburbs, EstateKit will run Meta ads that follow Meta's advertising policies (including special ad category rules where they apply), build and host a branded landing page and qualification form, send each qualified lead's details to your WhatsApp, give you dashboard access, send you a written update every week, and hold a check-in call with you at least once a month. The weekly check-in form is how you give us feedback on the ads, and we make changes from it each week.

Your first ads go live within 7 business days after you have paid, completed the onboarding form, given us access to your Facebook page, Instagram and ad account, and set up payment for your ad spend. We send you the ads to approve before they go live. The day they go live is the **launch date**.

We do not call or book appointments with leads; contacting leads is your job. Your leads go only to you or your nominated agent.

### Your areas

While this agreement runs, we will not run seller ads for any other agent that target or name your target suburbs. Meta shows ads by location and radius, so ads for an agent in a neighbouring suburb may reach some people in yours, and some of your leads may come from just outside your suburbs. Those leads are still yours.

You may swap one suburb for another available suburb once every 30 days by email or WhatsApp. Your suburbs are released when the agreement ends.

### Fees and ad spend

You pay the fee for your plan by EFT to the account on our invoice, on the payment schedule in the client details. Monthly plans are paid before each 30-day cycle. We start work once your first payment is in.

Ad spend is separate and must be at least the minimum daily amount in the client details. You pay Meta directly, or, if the client details say so, you pay us and we pay Meta at cost with no markup and refund any unspent balance when the agreement ends.

If a payment is more than 5 business days late, we may pause your ads until it is paid. Fees are not refundable except under the cancellation rights below, or where the law requires.

### How long it runs and pauses

The agreement starts when both parties sign. The **90 days** start on the launch date and are extended by one day for every day the ads can't run because of something on your side, such as unpaid ad spend or missing access.

You may pause your ads for up to 30 days in total during the 90 days, for example over a holiday, by giving us 5 business days' notice by email or WhatsApp. The 90 days (or your current 30-day cycle on a monthly plan) are extended by the length of the pause, at no extra fee, and paused days don't count toward the Lead Guarantee.

The 3-month package ends after the 90 days unless we agree in writing to continue. Monthly plans renew every 30 days; either side can cancel by giving 7 days' notice before the end of a cycle.

### Your part

For the whole 90 days (and the whole agreement on a monthly plan), you must:

- give us admin access to your Facebook page, Instagram and ad account within 5 business days of signing;
- keep ad spend at or above the minimum, with no more than 2 days of lapses in total (agreed pauses don't count);
- call at least 90% of leads within 15 minutes when they arrive between 08:00 and 18:00, and by 09:00 the next day otherwise;
- make at least 8 call or WhatsApp attempts over at least 7 days before giving up on a lead you can't reach;
- attend, or reschedule in advance, every appointment booked with a lead;
- mark each lead's outcome on the dashboard within 48 hours (a short note is enough);
- fill in the weekly check-in form for at least 10 of the 13 weeks;
- reply to our approval requests within 2 business days, and not edit, pause or delete the ads or landing pages yourself; and
- hold a valid PPRA registration and Fidelity Fund Certificate, and follow POPIA, the Property Practitioners Act and the Consumer Protection Act when dealing with leads.

If a nominated agent works your leads, these duties apply to them, and you remain responsible for them being met.

Dashboard records, WhatsApp delivery times and Meta records are the main evidence of whether you did your part. You may also send us your own records, such as call logs or WhatsApp screenshots, and we will take them into account.

### Lead Guarantee

A **qualified lead** is a homeowner in or near your target suburbs who gives us their property address, a selling timeline and a working phone number through your form. Duplicates don't count, and neither do leads you report to us as fake or with a wrong number within 14 days of receiving them.

We guarantee you at least **60 qualified leads** during the 90 days. The guarantee applies when your ad spend stayed at or above the minimum, you gave us access and replied to approval requests on time, you didn't edit, pause or delete the ads or landing pages yourself, your ads were never paused for late payment, and you didn't cancel early. Days the ads were paused don't count toward the 90 days.

If we fall short:

- **We work for free:** we keep managing your ads with no management fee until you reach 60 qualified leads. You keep paying ad spend during this time.
- **You can check it yourself:** every lead shows on your dashboard with the date it came in.

You don't need to claim it. Within 7 days after the 90 days end, we tell you in writing whether you reached 60, and if not, the free period starts the next day. This guarantee covers the number of leads only. What those leads turn into depends on your follow-up and the market (see "No other guarantees").

### No other guarantees

Apart from the Lead Guarantee, we do not guarantee any number of leads, appointments, mandates, sales or commission. Results depend on the market, your areas, your ad spend and your follow-up. The ads run on Meta, and we are not responsible for Meta's rejections, restrictions, outages or price changes, though we will work to fix them. Results we have shown you from other agents are examples, not promises.

### Confidentiality and access

Each side keeps the other's confidential information private and uses it only for this agreement. Yours includes your clients, listings, leads, business figures and anything you share on our calls. Ours includes our templates, ad setups, pricing and systems. This lasts during the agreement and for 2 years after it ends, and binds everyone on our team who works on your account.

We use the access you give us only to run your campaign. We do not post on your page, message your contacts, or change your payment methods. On a calendar you connect, we only check when you are free and add bookings; you may connect a separate booking calendar instead of your personal one. We remove all our access within 5 business days after the agreement ends.

### Data and ownership

You own your leads, brand, Facebook page, Instagram and ad account. Under POPIA you are the responsible party and we process lead data only on your behalf. Your landing page shows a privacy notice and asks each lead's consent to be contacted by you. We keep lead data secure, tell you straight away about any breach, and use providers (Meta, WhatsApp, hosting and dashboard providers, some outside South Africa) that give adequate data protection. Within 30 days after the agreement ends, we give you an export of your leads on request and then delete them.

We own our templates, ad frameworks, copy, dashboard and software; you may use the ads and pages made for you while this agreement runs. If the client details say Yes, we may use your name, results and testimonials in our marketing, but never a lead's details, and you can withdraw this at any time by email. We charge a fixed fee, take no commission and do not act as a property practitioner. For 12 months after the agreement ends, you will not hire anyone from our team who worked on your account without our consent.

### Liability

As far as the law allows, our total liability is limited to the fees you paid in the 3 months before a claim, and we are not liable for indirect losses such as lost commission or listings. You are responsible for any claim arising from how you or your nominated agent contact or treat leads. Nothing here limits liability for fraud, gross negligence or anything the Consumer Protection Act does not allow to be limited.

### Ending the agreement

Either side may end the agreement if the other breaks it and doesn't fix it within 7 days of written notice. Where the Consumer Protection Act or Electronic Communications and Transactions Act gives you a cooling-off right, you may cancel within 5 business days of signing (or longer if the Act allows) and get your fees back, but not ad spend already paid to Meta. Where the Consumer Protection Act applies, you may also cancel the 3-month package early on 20 business days' notice and get a pro rata refund for the unused days, less R1,500 for set-up work. When the agreement ends, we stop the ads, take down the pages, release your suburbs and remove our access to your accounts.

### General

South African law applies. We will first try to settle any dispute by talking for 10 business days; after that either side may go to the Magistrate's Court, and you keep any right to approach the National Consumer Commission or the Information Regulator. This document is the whole agreement. We use the same terms for every client, so we don't make one-off changes to them; any change must be in writing and signed by both sides, and electronic signatures count. Notices go to the email addresses in the client details. If any part is unenforceable, the rest still applies. Anyone signing for a company confirms they are authorised to do so.
