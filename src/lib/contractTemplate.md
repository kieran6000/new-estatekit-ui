### What we do

For your target areas, EstateKit will run Meta ads under the Housing Special Ad Category, build and host a branded landing page and qualification form, send each qualified lead's details to your WhatsApp, give you CRM access, send a written update every week, and hold a check-in call every 14 days to adjust the ads.

Your first ads go live within 7 business days after you have paid, completed the onboarding form, given us access to your Facebook page, Instagram and ad account, and set up payment for your ad spend. The day they go live is the **launch date**.

We do not call or book appointments with leads; contacting leads is your job. Your leads go only to you. We may run ads for other agents in the same areas.

### Fees and ad spend

You pay the fee for your plan by EFT in advance: the 3-month package in full before launch, monthly plans before each 30-day cycle. We start work once your first payment is in.

Ad spend is separate and must be at least the minimum daily amount above. You pay Meta directly, or, if the details above say so, you pay us and we pay Meta at cost with no markup and refund any unspent balance when the agreement ends.

If a fee is more than 5 business days late, we may pause your ads until it is paid. Fees are not refundable except under the Mandate Guarantee, the cancellation rights below, or where the law requires.

### How long it runs

The agreement starts when both parties sign. The **90 days** start on the launch date and are extended by one day for every day the ads can't run because of something on your side, such as unpaid ad spend or missing access. The 3-month package ends after the 90 days unless we agree in writing to continue. Monthly plans renew every 30 days; either side can cancel by giving 7 days' notice before the end of a cycle.

### Your part

For the whole 90 days (and the whole agreement on a monthly plan), you must:

- give us admin access to your Facebook page, Instagram and ad account within 5 business days of signing;
- keep ad spend at or above the minimum, with no more than 2 days of lapses in total;
- call at least 90% of leads within 15 minutes when they arrive between 08:00 and 18:00, and by 09:00 the next day otherwise;
- make at least 8 call or WhatsApp attempts over at least 7 days before giving up on a lead you can't reach;
- attend, or reschedule in advance, every appointment booked with a lead;
- log every contact attempt and outcome in the CRM within 48 hours;
- fill in the weekly check-in form for at least 10 of the 13 weeks;
- reply to our approval requests within 2 business days, and not edit, pause or delete the ads or landing pages yourself; and
- hold a valid PPRA registration and Fidelity Fund Certificate and follow POPIA, the Property Practitioners Act and the Consumer Protection Act when dealing with leads.

CRM records, WhatsApp delivery times and Meta records are the main evidence of whether you did your part. You may also send us your own records, such as call logs, and we will take them into account.

### Mandate Guarantee

A **mandate** is any sole or open mandate, or other signed listing or sale agreement, with a lead we sent you (or that lead's spouse or co-owner).

If fewer than 2 mandates are signed during the 90 days, you qualify for the guarantee when all of these are true: you paid the 3-month package, or 3 months in a row on time on a monthly plan; you did everything under "Your part" for the full 90 days; your ads were never paused for late fees; you didn't cancel early; and you haven't claimed this guarantee before.

If you qualify, you choose one of these:

**Refund:** 50% of the management fees paid for the 90 days (R3,750 on the 3-month package). Ad spend is not refunded.

**Free extension:** we keep managing your ads with no fee for 1 additional month. You must keep paying ad spend and doing your part during the extension, or it ends.

To claim, email us within 14 days after the 90 days end. We will reply in writing with our decision and reasons within 10 business days. An approved refund is paid within 14 days; an approved extension starts the next day. If, within 60 days after we pay a refund, a mandate is signed that brings you to 2, you must repay the refund within 14 days.

### No other guarantees

Apart from the Mandate Guarantee, we do not guarantee any number of leads, appointments, mandates, sales or commission. Results depend on the market, your areas, your ad spend and your follow-up. The ads run on Meta, and we are not responsible for Meta's rejections, restrictions, outages or price changes, though we will work to fix them. Results we have shown you from other agents are examples, not promises.

### Liability

As far as the law allows, our total liability is limited to the fees you paid in the 3 months before a claim, and we are not liable for indirect losses such as lost commission or listings. You are responsible for any claim arising from how you contact or treat leads. Nothing here limits liability for fraud, gross negligence or anything the Consumer Protection Act does not allow to be limited.

### Data and ownership

You own your leads, brand, Facebook page, Instagram and ad account. Under POPIA you are the responsible party and we process lead data only on your behalf. Your landing page shows a privacy notice and asks each lead's consent to be contacted by you. We keep lead data secure, tell you straight away about any breach, and use providers (Meta, WhatsApp, hosting and CRM, some outside South Africa) that give adequate data protection. Within 30 days after the agreement ends, we give you an export of your leads on request and then delete them.

We own our templates, ad frameworks, copy, CRM and software; you may use the ads and pages made for you while this agreement runs. We may use your name, results and testimonials in our marketing, but never a lead's details, and you can withdraw this by email. We charge a fixed fee, take no commission and do not act as a property practitioner. For 12 months after the agreement ends, you will not hire anyone from our team who worked on your account without our consent.

### Ending the agreement

Either side may end the agreement if the other breaks it and doesn't fix it within 7 days of written notice. Where the Consumer Protection Act or Electronic Communications and Transactions Act gives you a cooling-off right, you may cancel within 5 business days of signing (or longer if the Act allows) and get your fees back, but not ad spend already paid to Meta. Where the Consumer Protection Act applies, you may also cancel the 3-month package early on 20 business days' notice and get a pro rata refund for the unused days, less R1,500 for set-up work. When the agreement ends, we stop the ads, take down the pages and remove our access to your accounts.

### General

South African law applies. We will first try to settle any dispute by talking for 10 business days; after that either side may go to the Magistrate's Court, and you keep any right to approach the National Consumer Commission or the Information Regulator.
